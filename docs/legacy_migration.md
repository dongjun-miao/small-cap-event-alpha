# Legacy-to-Canonical Migration

The original exploratory notebooks used inconsistent definitions:

- notebook 06 assigned market-cap terciles over each complete five-year file;
- notebook 07 assigned terciles within each market date;
- rolling-volume baselines differed on whether the event day was included;
- forward-return labels differed between future close and future open exits.

The `v2` notebooks supersede those files. They do not duplicate signal logic;
all definitions are imported from `src/event_alpha/core.py` and all full-sample
outputs are generated by `scripts/run_research.py`.

Canonical changes:

1. daily cross-sectional size terciles;
2. rolling volume and ADV shifted through `t-1`;
3. next-session-open entry and `t+h` close exit;
4. exact market-session validation;
5. period-relative mechanism buckets;
6. mean, median, and winsorized results reported together.

As a result, legacy headline figures should not be quoted. The canonical
20-day gross mean range is **1.74%--13.94%**, and the range after a simple
100 bp stress is **0.74%--12.94%** across the five subperiods.
